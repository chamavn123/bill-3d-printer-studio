Official BambuStudio 0.4 mm start/end templates for A1, A1 mini and P1S. Source: https://github.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/tree/47a13eeb1d9af65ef2371e361c856e21d4f6ca72
Original templates are bundled in templates.json. generator.js optionally changes first standby temperature, removes melody commands, and adds waits or a timed part-fan cycle. See BambuStudio-LICENSE.txt.

Front-edge push movements are generated by Nozzle Studio from form options, not part of the original Bambu templates. Experimental; no hardware validation performed.
