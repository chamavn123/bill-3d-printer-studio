    FROM SETTINGS TO SLICER
    Make your next print your own.
    1. Choose your exact printer. A1, A1 mini, and P1S templates here are for a 0.4 mm nozzle.
    2. Configure Start G-code. Set standby nozzle temperature, waits around cleaning, and bed soak time. Print temperatures still come from your filament profile.
    3. Configure End G-code. Choose a height above or below the highest or first layer. Height-only movement keeps factory X/Y; hovering over the print requires at least 2 mm above the highest layer. Choose fan percentages and cooling minutes, or an experimental bed-temperature target.
    4. Copy each routine separately. Duplicate your printer preset in Bambu Studio. Open the printer preset editor → Machine G-code, then paste into Machine start G-code or Machine end G-code, matching the selected tab.
    5. Save and slice. These are slicer templates containing variables. Process them in Bambu Studio; never run the downloaded text directly on the printer. Review the sliced result and supervise the first test.
    For optional front-edge push, choose a contact height, clearance behind the model, speed, and stopping point. The app generates the path. Use one straight-sided, already-released part with no brim, supports, or other objects, and a clear front exit. This experimental sweep does not guarantee removal. The first-layer reference is the nominal first-layer Z, not the lowest surface of an arbitrary model. Temperature waits may reheat a cold bed and have no timeout. Use your filament profile for print temperatures. Compare the linked template version with your installed Bambu Studio and firmware. This site generates text and does not connect to your printer.
